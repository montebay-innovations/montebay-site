Where apps usually get stuck

It works in a browser, not as an app

Apple turns away apps that are only a website in a frame. We add the native pieces that make it a real app: push notifications, the camera, health data, widgets, offline use.

Apple rejected it

We read the rejection, find what the reviewer actually saw, fix it, and write the reply. Most rejections come down to a short list of rules we have already worked through ourselves.

Payments don’t work in the app

Apple and Google require their own billing for digital subscriptions. We set it up on both stores, including the restore and renewal cases that quietly break.

Questions

Will Apple accept a web app wrapped as an app?

Apple rejects apps that are only a website in a frame, under guideline 4.2. An app that adds real native value usually passes. In the free review we tell you how your app looks against that rule before you pay for anything.

Can you work with an app built in Cursor, Lovable or Replit?

Yes. If the code can be exported to a repository, we can work with it. AI-built apps often need cleanup in the same few places, and we point those out in the review.

Who owns the code and the store listings?

You do. The work happens in your repository and under your own Apple and Google developer accounts.

Do I need my own developer accounts?

Yes. The Apple Developer Program is $99 a year and a Google Play developer account is a one-time $25. We can walk you through setting both up.

Why does Android take longer?

Google requires new personal developer accounts to run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row before an app can go public. We plan the schedule around it and help you organize testers. Organization accounts are exempt, and we can tell you whether switching makes sense.

What if my app was already rejected?

Send us the rejection message. That is exactly what the App Store rescue package is for.
